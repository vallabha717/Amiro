Amiro has three voice surfaces: dictation for writing, read-aloud for hearing replies, and a hands-free live voice room. You set the providers once on the **Admin → Voice** page, then use any surface.

<img src="/docs/assets/docs/demo-voice-setup.gif" alt="Setting up voice: the Voice and Speech page with text-to-speech and speech-to-text providers, read-aloud toggles, and the voice picker" width="100%" />

*Setting up voice: providers on one page, read-aloud toggles, and the voice picker. The steps below walk the same path.*

## Do it yourself

The steps below walk the same path the clip shows: pick the two providers, preview a voice, save, and turn on read-aloud.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the voice page">
Click **Admin** in the sidebar, then **Voice**. The page is owner-only, so only the admin account can change providers or store keys here.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick a text-to-speech provider">
Under **Text to speech**, choose the provider that reads replies out loud. Amiro ships a catalog of seven.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick a speech-to-text provider">
Under **Speech to text**, choose the provider that turns your microphone into text for dictation.
</Step>
<Step title="Paste your keys">
For any provider that needs a key, paste it here. Keys go into the encrypted credential vault and are shown back only as a masked hint.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose a model and voice">
Pick a model and a voice for each provider. Where the provider supports it, use the button to fetch its live voice list so you can choose from real voices.
</Step>
<Step title="Preview before you commit">
Click **Test Voice** to hear the configured voice, or play an individual voice chip to compare. Do this before saving so you know it sounds right.
</Step>
<Step title="Save">
Save the page. Your choices take effect immediately.
</Step>
<Step title="Turn on read-aloud">
In the output behavior card, toggle **Read Aloud** and auto read-aloud. Auto read-aloud speaks each finished reply without you clicking anything.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Which path fits you

| If you want... | Use this path | What you need ready |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Good-quality cloud voices | **API key provider** (OpenAI, ElevenLabs, Deepgram, Fish Audio, xAI) | The provider's key, pasted on this page |
| Free, offline speech with no key | **Browser speech** (`browser-native`) | Nothing. Your browser and OS decide which voices exist and whether they work offline |
| Your own speech server | **Local endpoint** (custom OpenAI-compatible, for example 9Router or LiteLLM) | The address where the server listens |
| Hands-free two-way talking | **ElevenLabs** | An ElevenLabs key. Live voice always uses ElevenLabs, for speech in and speech out |

The browser-native path needs no key. Amiro hands synthesis and recognition to your browser, so the voices and languages you get depend on your browser and operating system.

## Verify it worked

- Click **Test Voice** and hear the sentence spoken. If you hear it, text-to-speech is working.
- Click the mic button in the composer and say a few words. The transcript should land in the composer for you to edit before sending.
- Click **Read aloud** on any assistant message and hear it play, then try **Stop reading**.
- Turn on auto read-aloud and send a message. The reply should be spoken when the turn finishes.

## If it does not work

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="There is no sound when I test a voice">
- Check that a text-to-speech provider is selected and, if it needs a key, that the key is saved. A keyless browser provider still depends on your browser and OS having voices installed.
- Make sure the model and voice you picked belong to the selected provider.
- Turn your system volume up and confirm the browser tab is not muted.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The microphone button does nothing">
- Give the browser permission to use the microphone. Look for the permission prompt near the address bar and allow it.
- Check that a speech-to-text provider is selected.
- If you are using a server provider, the audio is recorded and sent for transcription. A very short clip may come back empty, so speak for a second or two.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Live voice will not start">
- Live voice needs a configured ElevenLabs key, microphone permission, and a browser that supports the audio path. The preflight check reports a plain-language reason if something is missing.
- If you are on the browser-native provider for dictation, note that live voice ignores it and always uses ElevenLabs.
- When a live session is running, auto read-aloud is paused on purpose, because the live session already speaks every answer.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Auto read-aloud stays silent">
- Confirm that both the read-aloud action and auto read-aloud are turned on in the output behavior card.
- Auto read-aloud is skipped while a live voice session is active. End the call and try again.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Live voice room

Live voice is hands-free: you talk, the server finds the end of your sentence, the agent answers, and the answer is spoken as it is written.

<img src="/docs/assets/readme/voice-room.png" alt="Voice room: the plush mascot listening, with speaker, mic, stop-answer and end-call controls" width="100%" />

*The voice room: your plush mascot listens, with speaker, mic, stop-answer, and end-call controls.*

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a session">
    Press Cmd+Shift+I (or Ctrl+Shift+I), or click the voice mode button in the composer or header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Talk">
    The room appears above the composer, with the agent's plush mascot reacting to your microphone. Speak normally and pause to finish a turn.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Interrupt if you need to">
    Speak over the reply to barge in. The current answer is cancelled and the agent listens to you instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish">
    Click End voice, press the shortcut again, or use the room controls. Minimizing leaves a small dock above the composer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Cmd+Shift+I or Ctrl+Shift+I | Start or end voice |
| Space | Finish your turn while listening, or stop the answer while it is thinking or speaking |
| M | Toggle your microphone mute |
| Esc | Minimize the room without ending the call |
