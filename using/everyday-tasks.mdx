Surveys of how people actually use AI assistants keep landing on the same
short list: **looking things up**, **summarising**, **writing and email**, and
**keeping on top of a schedule**. This page shows the first three with prompts
you can paste as they are. [Schedules](/using/schedules) covers the fourth.

<Info>
Web research needs a search engine. Bing and DuckDuckGo work with no key: pick one in
the setup chat, or in **Admin → Search Providers → Add**. See [Search](/using/search).
</Info>

## Research on the live web

<Frame caption="Amiro searches, reads the pages, and answers with a table and its sources. The follow-up keeps the context.">
  <video controls muted playsInline preload="metadata" poster="/docs/assets/showcase/research.jpg" src="/docs/assets/showcase/research.mp4" />
</Frame>

```text
Search the web and compare Obsidian, Logseq and Joplin for personal notes today.
Give me a table with license, price, sync options and mobile apps, then one line
on who each one suits. Cite the pages you used.
```

What to look for:

- The **search** and **fetch** tool calls fold into a single "work" row above
  the answer. Click it to see every query and page Amiro opened.
- The **Tools** menu in the chat header hides tool calls you do not want to
  see, down to answers only.
- Ask a follow-up in the same chat. The previous answer stays in context, so
  "which one, if I write mostly on my phone?" just works.

More prompts that work well:

```text
What changed in the latest stable Rust release? Three bullets, with the link to the release notes.
```

```text
Find three flights or trains from Bengaluru to Goa next Friday evening and list price, duration and operator.
```

## Summarise a page and keep the notes

<Frame caption="Give a link and a job. The summary lands in chat and as a Markdown file in the agent's workspace.">
  <video controls muted playsInline preload="metadata" poster="/docs/assets/showcase/documents.jpg" src="/docs/assets/showcase/documents.mp4" />
</Frame>

```text
Read https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pomodoro_Technique and summarise it in 5 bullets.
Then write a Pomodoro plan for my afternoon (2pm to 6pm) and save both to
notes/pomodoro.md in your workspace.
```

Then open **Agent → Workspace** (or click the file chip in the reply) to read,
edit or download the file. You can also:

- **Attach files** with the paperclip: PDFs, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, CSV,
  images, audio and video all open in the built-in viewer.
- Type **`@`** in the composer to point at a file or folder in the chat's
  folder.
- Drop a long passage straight into the composer; it stays inline as text.

## Chart real numbers

<Frame caption="Numbers from the web, drawn inline with the Visualize superpower.">
  <video controls muted playsInline preload="metadata" poster="/docs/assets/showcase/visualize.jpg" src="/docs/assets/showcase/visualize.mp4" />
</Frame>

```text
Search the web for India's total installed solar power capacity (GW) at the end
of each year from 2019 to 2024, then use the visualize skill to draw it as an
inline bar chart with a one-line takeaway. Mention your source.
```

Type **`/vis`** in the composer to attach the Visualize skill explicitly.
Charts, diagrams (Mermaid) and small interactive widgets render right in the
reply.

## Write for you

```text
Draft a polite email to my landlord: the kitchen tap has been leaking for a week
and I'd like a plumber to come before Saturday. Keep it short.
```

```text
Turn these meeting notes into a summary with decisions, owners and dates: …
```

Amiro writes in the voice you set under [Personality](/using/personalizing-your-agent),
and anything you tell it to remember (your name, your sign-off, your timezone)
carries into every chat through [Memory](/using/memory).
