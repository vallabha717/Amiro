The agent has a name, a face, and a personality you control. Everything here lives on the Agent hub at `/agent`.

## Open the Agent hub

Click **Agent** in the sidebar, or go to `/agent`. The hub is built around one clickable avatar with the four quick-jump pills: Personality, Superpowers, Memory, and Workspace.

## Name, tagline, and portrait

1. Click the central avatar, or the Customize control on the hub.
2. Edit the display name inline, or use the rename control.
3. Set a tagline.
4. Pick one of the plush characters.
5. Optionally upload a local portrait image.

Your saved name is used in the sidebar heading and in the chat surfaces. An uploaded portrait keeps its original colors.

## Pick a character

The characters are OpenDots plush mascots. The setup chat offers four looks: **Headphones**, **Sprout**, **Starfish**, and **Dreamer**. The picker presents them as distinct plush characters, and a Midnight theme renders a black plush variant with its shading and highlights preserved.

The character tint follows the active sidebar theme, including light and dark changes. Selecting a character or theme in the customizer updates the agent, and selecting a theme from the sidebar user menu also saves that color to the agent, so the choice survives a reload.

<Note>
The avatars are decorative. They are not a live activity indicator, and the habitat animation pauses on hover and when you press its pause control. If your system has reduced motion enabled, decorative movement stops.
</Note>

## Tune the personality

Open **Personality** from the hub or go to `/agent/personality`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start from a preset (optional)">
    Pick one of the preset personas to set all five sliders at once, for example Sharp Operator, Warm Companion, Unfiltered Co-Founder, Balanced Neutral, Midnight Strategist, Studio Dreamer, or Field Engineer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust the sliders">
    The five axes are Companion to Coworker, Gen Z to Baby Boomer, Independent to Collaborative, Playful to Serious, and Polite to Unfiltered. Each runs from 0 to 100 with a balanced midpoint.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the live preview">
    The preview sentence is an illustrative sample of the dial settings. It is written on the page, not generated by the model and not a voice recording.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click the update control. Saving rewrites the agent's identity file and updates the summary shown across the app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Personality sliders change writing style only. They do not change permissions and they do not grant autonomy. Skills, tools, and schedules are configured separately.
</Warning>

## What saving does

- The personality summary is stored per user under the app home and mirrored into the agent's `IDENTITY.md`, which the runtime reads on every turn.
- Saving a personality change rewrites `IDENTITY.md` but never touches the other home files such as `NOW.md`.
- The sidebar accent color persists to the agent's color trait, so the sidebar and the agent colors stay in sync in both directions.
