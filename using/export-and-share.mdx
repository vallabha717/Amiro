---

You can share a single thread as a link, keep exports you made, and group threads into named archives.

## Export a thread

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the thread menu">
    Open the conversation you want to share, then open its thread menu (the same menu that holds Rename).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Export Chat">
    Select **Export Chat**. A dialog opens with the share link controls.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the link">
    Click **Create Link**. Amiro captures the conversation, copies the link to your clipboard, and opens the export page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it">
    Send the copied link. It points at the export's own page, which shows the conversation in a read-only preview with its title, messages, and exporter name.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The export is a snapshot. Later messages in the original thread do not appear in an export you already created, and deleting the original thread does not delete the export. If you did not set an expiry, the export has none and stays until you delete it.
</Note>

## Manage your exports

Open Chat Preferences and find **My Exports**. It lists the exports you created with their title, export time, expiry if any, and comment count. From there you can open an export or delete it.

- The export page shows the conversation read-only.
- Signed-in users can comment on an export, and replies nest under their parent comment.
- Deleting an export removes the shareable copy. It does not touch the original thread.

<Warning>
Anyone with the link can open the export page, so treat an export link like a shared document. If the conversation contains sensitive content, do not create a link for it, and delete an existing export when you are done sharing.
</Warning>

## Group threads into archives

An archive is a named collection of threads, useful for keeping related conversations together after they leave the active list.

### Create an archive

1. In the sidebar, find the **Archive** entry.
2. Click its plus control to open the new archive dialog.
3. Give it a name and an optional description, then save.

### Add a thread to an archive

1. Open the thread menu for the conversation.
2. Choose **Add to archive**.
3. Pick an archive from the list, or create one first if the list is empty.

### Open and edit an archive

Click the archive in the sidebar to open its page. It lists the threads in that archive and shows when it was created. Use the archive actions to rename or edit its description, or to delete it. Deleting an archive removes the grouping, not the underlying conversations.

<Note>
Archives are organizational. Adding a thread to an archive does not delete it or hide it from your other views. Use the delete actions in the sidebar when you actually want a conversation gone.
</Note>
