Amiro tells you when something finished while you were away: a long reply, a
schedule run, a note from the heartbeat. Everything lands in the **Bell** in
the top bar; you can also have it delivered outside Amiro.

## The Bell

- The badge counts unread notifications. The popover opens on **Unread**;
  turn the switch off to see everything.
- Click a row for its detail and delivery state, then **Go to conversation**
  to open the thread it came from.
- The **…** menu has **Mark all read** and **Settings** (sound, browser
  notifications).

## When a chat notifies you

A reply you are watching never notifies you. If you start something long and
leave (open Schedules, another chat, or close the tab), the reply still
finishes; you get a Bell notification and a green dot next to the chat in the
list. Opening the chat clears both.

## Deliver outside Amiro

Important notifications can also reach you on **Telegram, Slack or Discord**:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect a bot in Channels">
    **Agent → Channels**: pick the app and paste the bot token into its secure
    field (for Telegram, the token from @BotFather). It goes to the vault and
    Amiro checks the bot before saving.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your own account to your contact card">
    **Agent → Contacts → you**: add your Telegram, Slack or Discord account and
    verify it with the code Amiro sends there. Unverified accounts never get
    deliveries.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what goes where">
    **Admin → Notifications**: pick which events notify you and your quiet
    hours, then **Send a test notification**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Routine updates stay in Amiro. Urgent things that need you, and test
notifications, go to every ready channel. Chat replies always stay in Amiro.
Channels also decide who may talk *to* your agent on those apps, with trust
levels from *no one* to *verified contacts*.
