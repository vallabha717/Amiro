---
title: "Voice"
description: "Dictate messages, have replies read aloud, and hold live voice conversations with your agent."
---

Amiro speaks and listens in three ways: dictating into the composer, reading replies aloud, and full live voice conversations.

## Dictating a message

Use the microphone button in the chat composer. It transcribes through your configured speech recognition provider, and the text lands in the composer where you can edit it before sending.

## Reading replies aloud

Each assistant message has a speaker action that reads it back through your configured text to speech voice. You can also turn on automatic read aloud so replies are spoken as soon as the turn finishes. Read aloud pauses while a live voice session is active.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open voice settings">
    Go to Admin, Voice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your voices">
    Choose a text to speech provider and voice for narration, and a speech to text provider for dictation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview and save">
    Play the voice preview to check it sounds right, then save. Dictation and read aloud use these defaults across Amiro.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Live voice conversations

Live voice is a hands free mode: streamed microphone audio goes to the server, which transcribes it and streams spoken replies back, with interruption support so you can talk over the agent. Start it from the voice button in the composer or the app header.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a voice session">
    Open a chat and press the voice button. The voice room opens above the composer with your agent's plush mascot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Talk naturally">
    Speak, and your words appear as captions while the agent replies out loud. Interrupt any time by speaking over it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Minimize or end">
    Minimize the room to keep talking while you browse, or end the session to return to text. Transcripts land in the chat thread.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Live voice uses its own streaming speech path rather than the dictation and read aloud providers, so it works even if you have not configured those.
</Tip>

## If voice is not working

- Check microphone permission in your browser first.
- Open Admin, Voice and confirm both providers are configured.
- On a restrictive network, plain dictation and read aloud may work where live voice does not, since live voice holds a streaming connection.
