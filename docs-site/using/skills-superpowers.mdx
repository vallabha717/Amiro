---
title: "Skills and Superpowers"
description: "Find instruction sets, attach them to a message, and understand what skills can and cannot do."
---

Superpowers are instruction sets your agent can follow: the runtime's built-in skills, project skills, and instruction sets you write yourself.

## Using a skill in chat

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Superpowers surface">
    Go to the Agent hub and open Superpowers, or open the mention picker in the chat composer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the skill">
    Search the compact skill list for the one that matches your task.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it to your message">
    Choose Add to message. The skill's instructions travel with that message so the agent follows them for its reply.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Limits

- You can attach up to eight skills to one message.
- Very large skill selections are refused with an explanation instead of being silently cut.

## What skills can and cannot do

Skills supply instructions, not permissions. A skill does not enable new tools, open new data sources, or broaden what the agent is allowed to touch. If a skill you select is unsupported or disabled, it is rejected with a reason rather than silently substituted.

## Writing your own

You can author your own instruction sets, which then appear in the same catalog as the built-in ones. They are stored under your own user area and resolve from your catalog when you select them.
