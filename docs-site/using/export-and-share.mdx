## Exporting a thread

Export a conversation to keep a copy outside Amiro. The export captures the thread so you can store it as a file or pass it along. Your exports are listed for you to revisit, and you can delete an export you no longer need.

## Grouping chats into archives

Archives are named collections of threads you can reopen later. Group related conversations, for example everything from one project, so they stay reachable together even after the sidebar moves on.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Archives">
    Go to Archives from the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create an archive">
    Make a named archive for the topic or project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add threads">
    Add the threads that belong in it. Opening the archive later shows those conversations together.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Cleaning up

Delete a single thread from its menu when it has served its purpose. To clear the decks, the sidebar menu can delete all chats, or delete everything except what you archived. Deleting cannot be undone, so archive first when in doubt.

## Tips

- Export anything you might need outside Amiro before deleting the thread.
- Archive by project or by quarter so older work stays findable.
