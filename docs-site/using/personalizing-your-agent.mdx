Your agent lives at the **Agent** hub (`/agent`), reached from the sidebar. It has four areas: Personality, Superpowers, Memory, and Workspace. This page covers making it yours.

## Name and look

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Agent hub">
    Select your agent's entry in the sidebar to open `/agent`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the name and tagline">
    Give your agent a display name and a short tagline. The sidebar heading uses the name you choose.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a character and color">
    Choose one of the four plush characters and a sidebar theme color. The character tint follows the active sidebar theme, including light and dark mode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload a portrait (optional)">
    Upload your own image to replace the character. Uploaded portraits keep their original colors.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Personality

Personality has five bipolar style sliders plus presets. Move the sliders to taste and read the writing sample, which illustrates the current mix. Presets are handy starting points you can fine-tune.

<Note>
Personality sliders change how your agent sounds and writes. They do not change what it is allowed to do and they do not grant it autonomy.
</Note>

## Theme sync

The sidebar theme and the agent color stay in sync in both directions. Changing the theme in the sidebar user menu updates the agent, and saving a theme in the customizer updates the sidebar.

## Reduced motion

If you prefer reduced motion, the agent habitat respects your system setting and decorative movement pauses.
